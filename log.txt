[2026-09-22 11:31:05 IST] Landing page fixes after user review (version 4)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
CHANGED
  - apps/web/index.html
    * Headline: forgotten letters of "everything" now turn into hollow
      outlines instead of fading to near-invisible. User said the word
      was not readable.
    * Italic display type: pinned a sturdier optical size (opsz 26) and
      weight 500 on large Bodoni lines. Display-size hairlines were
      vanishing, so letters looked broken ("Il keeps", "servtce").
      Moved the rule to the end of the stylesheet because the font
      shorthand on those elements was resetting it.
    * Colour timing: a section now owns the colour while it holds the
      middle of the screen, blending only in a short band at each
      boundary. Before, colours ran a section ahead (amber barely seen,
      phones skipped the coral opening, pink bled into the reveal).
    * Colour blending moved from RGB to OKLCH so warm-to-cool steps
      pass through rose instead of muddy brown.
    * Final form section: pink (#FF3D6E) replaced with ultramarine
      (#1F2BD6) and white text. User did not like the pink.
    * Sun/moon halo: box-shadow replaced with a radial-gradient halo;
      removed the top bar background that cut across it. Both caused
      a visible hard-edged rectangle.
    * Drifting prompt fragments: dissolve near any readable element,
      and stop entirely at "Session ended." and the form.
    * Moon lowered in teal and memo sections so it no longer sits
      behind the kept counter. Poster kept clear of the sun. Phone
      headline wraps cleanly. Highlight padding tightened.

REMOVED (and why)
  - Pink form colour: user feedback.
  - Top bar background gradient: caused a hard band across the sun;
    text beneath it is already faded by the forgetting effect.

VERIFIED
  - Headless Chrome screenshots at 1440x900 and 400x860 of hero,
    story, blend midpoint, teal, memo, reveal and form.
